Memorable Moments.

The Green Man is a Portuguese and Brazilian dining destination on High Street in London. Explore a menu inspired by the flavours of Portugal and Brazil in a setting designed for sharing food, enjoying a drink, and spending time together.

Whether you are craving tender wood-seared Picanha steak, delicate Atlantic grilled sea bass with roasted potatoes, or cold Brazilian beer shared with close friends, our tavern bridges London's classic pub heritage with vibrant Iberian warmth.

Portuguese Heritage

Atlantic Seafood & Petiscos

Charcoal grilled, seasoned with lemon, sea salt & virgin olive oil

Experience the coastal soul of Portugal: whole grilled sea bass served on rustic wooden boards, grilled octopus (Polvo à Lagareiro) bathed in hot olive oil and garlic, crushed roasted potatoes, and traditional petiscos made for sharing across the table.

Brazilian Tradition

Brazilian Charcoal & Churrasco

Prime Picanha, cassava, hearty sides & tropical refreshers

Savour prime beef cuts prepared in the revered Brazilian churrasco style. Thick-cut Picanha with golden sea-salt crust, crispy cassava fries (Mandioca Frita), feijão preto, farofa, and chilled Guaraná Antarctica or handcrafted Caipirinhas.

Everything You Need To Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to all questions about visiting us at 109 High Street.

We are located at 109 High St, London NW10 4TS in the heart of Harlesden. We are conveniently situated near Willesden Junction and Harlesden stations, with frequent bus routes stopping right along High Street.
You can call our host desk directly on +44 20 8453 0661, reach out via our official Facebook page, or visit us in person.
While walk-ins are always welcomed subject to capacity, booking ahead is strongly advised for Friday and Saturday dinners, weekend live music performances, and Sunday family gatherings.
You can browse our interactive seasonal menu right above on this site! As we work with fresh daily seafood and seasonal prime butcher cuts, our in-restaurant specials blackboard also features daily catches and chef creations.
From Portugal, we feature charcoal grilled whole sea bass, Polvo à Lagareiro (grilled octopus), pastéis de bacalhau, and authentic regional wines. From Brazil, we serve sizzling Picanha steaks, coxinhas, hearty Feijoada, mandioca frita, and handcrafted Caipirinhas.
Live music sessions are generally hosted on weekend evenings (Friday and Saturday). Because performance calendars vary, we recommend calling the restaurant or checking our social channels for the specific weekend line-up.
The Green Man welcomes diners throughout the week from midday until 11:00 PM nightly.
Yes, collection and takeaway are available for selected items. Please ring the kitchen desk directly on 020 8453 0661 to place your order for timely collection.
We take allergies very seriously. Please make your server aware of any dietary needs or allergens before ordering, and note them in your table booking form. We prepare gluten-free and dairy-free options upon request.
